## 1. Introduction: The Steering Problem After Vedral

### 1.1 The Observer Effect, Precisely

The observer effect, in its classical form, is the claim that observing or measuring a system necessarily perturbs it. The textbook example is a tire-pressure gauge: the gauge must vent a small volume of air to register pressure, so the act of measuring the tire changes the tire. Formally, for a system state $s$ and measurement interaction $M$:

$$M(s) \neq s \quad \text{(measurement is non-identity)}$$

The seductive — and Vedral argues, wrong — inference is:

$$\text{Observer} \xrightarrow{\text{conscious attention}} \text{reality changes}$$

Vedral's dismantling has three moves, which IKD accepts as axiomatic inputs:

1. **Substrate monism.** The gauge and the air are the same kind of stuff. There is no interaction that involves "consciousness" as a term; there are only physical interactions. The observer is a physical system, and measurement is an ordinary dynamical coupling.
2. **No privileged collapse agent.** In the quantum formalism, "collapse" (or its decoherent modern replacement) is triggered by interaction and entanglement with an environment — not by awareness, intention, or phenomenality.
3. **The effect is thermodynamic, not mental.** What looks like "observation changes reality" is the macroscopic shadow of microscopic coupling, averaged over update timescales far below perception.

### 1.2 The Residual Problem

Vedral removes the conscious pilot. He does not say who — if anyone — is flying the plane. IKD formalizes the three live hypotheses:

| Hypothesis | Claim | IKD verdict |
|---|---|---|
| (a) No driver | Trajectories are unsteered; agency is pure epiphenomenon | Rejected: fails to explain predictive success of self-models |
| (b) A parallel self drives | An alternate-branch version of you controls your branch | Rejected: implies a privileged branch, violating holographic symmetry |
| (c) Committee steering | Infinite alternate "yous" tug the wheel simultaneously | **Adopted, refined**: the tugs are components of one field, weighted by coherence magnitude $\lvert CI^\alpha \rvert$ |

The refinement of (c) is the core move of this paper: *the committee is not a collection of agents; it is a basis expansion of a single operator.* The "infinite alternate selves" are correlation branches $\Psi^\alpha_{\text{base}}$ of one substrate, and "their" steering is a single non-local gradient flow decomposed into branch components.

### 1.3 The Central Thesis

> **Thesis (IKD).** Agency is a non-local gradient flow on a correlation manifold. The local self is a holographic boundary record ($CI_B$) of transitions computed by the informational continuum ($CI_C$). Free will exists only in the nullspace of the universe's context matrix; the feeling of control is a thermodynamically necessary regularized report of being pushed by a correlation gradient.
